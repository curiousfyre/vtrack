Autometer is a simple, private vehicle tracker for fuel economy and maintenance schedules. All data stays on your device.

Features:
* Track fuel fill-ups and calculate MPG with partial fill support
* Set up maintenance schedules (oil changes, tire rotations, etc.) by mileage interval
* Dashboard showing fuel economy trends and upcoming maintenance
* Maintenance urgency indicators (OK, Due Soon, Overdue)
* Daily notifications when maintenance is due
* Support for multiple vehicles
* Statistics with fuel economy charts
* No account required, no cloud, no tracking
